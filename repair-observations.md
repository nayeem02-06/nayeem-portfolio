# Repair Observations

The fresh entry screen loads and its primary button removes the overlay. Immediately after that interaction, the browser capture can show the header over an otherwise blank hero even though the main hero text exists in the document. The first repair will therefore remove any opacity-based hero entrance state that can conceal essential content during the transition; the hero will remain visible independently of decorative motion and image loading.

The portfolio is a single long-form route with internal section anchors rather than separate pages. Its core audit covers the entry state, internal section links, language toggle, mobile menu, contact link, and external venture/social destinations.
